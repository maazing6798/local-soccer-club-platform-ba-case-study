# Change Management & Benefits Realisation

## 1. Change impact
| Group | What changes for them | Impact | How we prepare them |
|-------|------------------------|:-:|---------------------|
| Treasurer | No more bank matching or chasing; reviews fee support requests and exports | High (positive) | 1-hour walkthrough, quick-reference sheet |
| Registrar | Works from an exceptions list instead of spreadsheets | High (positive) | 1-hour walkthrough; on call during registration week |
| Child safety officer | Verifies WWCCs in the portal; gets expiry alerts | Medium | 30-minute walkthrough |
| Coaches & team managers | Announcements and availability move from WhatsApp to the app | High | Pre-season coaches' night; 2 pilot teams first |
| Parents | Register and pay in the app; leave the WhatsApp groups | High | Email, flyer at training, help desk at registration day |
| Players | Optional view-only profile | Low | Parents set it up |

## 3. Hypercare
- **Release 1:** the BA and web lead are on call for the first 2 weeks of registrations (1–14 Dec), with a daily 10-minute check-in with the registrar.
- **Release 2:** the BA attends the first two Saturdays at the ground to help parents and coaches.

## 6. Lessons learned (mid-project retro)
- **What worked:** turning business rules into worked examples during refinement, such as the sibling discount in US-102, caught ambiguity before it reached the code.
- **What worked:** holding demos at the clubroom on training nights got far better feedback than video calls.
- **What to improve:** the federation's data limits should have been confirmed in discovery, not in sprint 1. External integrations now go on the discovery checklist.
- **What to improve:** a volunteer committee needs decisions batched into one weekly session. Ad-hoc questions sent during the week went unanswered.
